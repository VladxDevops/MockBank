# Patterns Discovered Over Time

* **Prompt Length Matters:** Shorter prompts often lead to better results when Gemma is given concise instructions. However, complex tasks require longer, more detailed prompts.
* **Few-Shot Examples are Key**: Utilizing the provided examples consistently produces more reliable outcomes. 
* **Context is Crucial**:  Providing sufficient context helps Gemma understand the task and generate relevant outputs effectively.
* **Regular Retraining Improves Performance:**  Retraining Gemma on fresh data improves its ability to handle new situations and tasks.
