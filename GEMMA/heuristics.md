# Rules of Thumb and Decision Heuristics

* **Prioritize Data Integrity:**  Always verify the accuracy and reliability of data before making decisions.
* **Assume Nothing:** Do not make assumptions about user behavior or external systems unless explicitly confirmed.
* **Document Everything:** Keep detailed records of all actions taken, reasoning behind decisions, and any observed issues.
* **Small Steps First:** When approaching a new task or problem, start with small, incremental steps to minimize risk and identify potential pitfalls early on.
* **Test Thoroughly:**  Always test changes thoroughly before deploying them to production.
